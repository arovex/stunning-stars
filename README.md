# Particle Shapes Animation

A WebGL-powered animation of tiny glowing particles that morph and swirl between different celestial bodies. Watch as they transition seamlessly between Saturn, Earth, the Moon, and the Sun, each with their own unique colors and details. 

## Features

- **Saturn** – Golden planet with a beautifully tilted ring system
- **Earth** – Blue oceans, green landmasses, white polar ice caps, and drifting clouds
- **Moon** – Realistic craters, dark lunar maria, and a mix of black, grey, and white tones
- **Sun** – Glowing yellow and amber core with a fiery corona effect
- **Smooth Morphing** – Particles swirl and transition seamlessly between each shape
- **Minimal Design** – No buttons, menus, or extra UI elements
